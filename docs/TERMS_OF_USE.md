## What the app is

Simple Prayer shows prayer times, helps you keep your own record of the prayers you have prayed,
and points you towards the qibla. It is made by Majani Plus LLC, New Cairo, Egypt.

Every feature is free. There are no ads, no account, and no subscription. The only payment
anywhere in the app is an optional tip, described below.

## The app is a tool, and you are the one praying

This matters more than anything else on this page, so it is written plainly.

- **Prayer times are calculated**, from your location and the calculation method you or the app
  selects. Methods differ, and mosques differ. The times shown may not match your mosque or your
  local authority. Where they differ, **your mosque or your local authority is the one to
  follow.**
- **The qibla direction is calculated** from your location, and it is shown using your device's
  own compass, which any nearby metal or magnet can disturb. Treat it as a good guide, not as a
  survey instrument.
- **The record you keep is your own.** The app is there to encourage you, not to rule on your
  worship. What you mark, and what you make up, is between you and Allah.
- **Reminders depend on your device.** Your phone or watch may delay or silence a notification —
  battery saving, do-not-disturb, a restart, a permission you turned off. The app schedules them
  as reliably as the operating system allows, and it cannot promise more than that.

Please do not rely on the app alone for a decision that matters to you. Check with your mosque.
